What would establish epitope survival?

An LC-MS time course measuring the intact parent and products retaining the selected target, with peptide chemistry, assay matrix and sampling conditions recorded. Serum preparation and anticoagulants can alter protease activity; serum estimates do not directly establish whole-blood or human circulation lifetimes.

Structural constraints, terminal changes, side groups and protein binding can prolong lifetime. These vaccine inputs assume unmodified linear L-peptides with free termini. Neither model establishes vaccine-fragment accuracy.

A cut inside the selected exact target splits it. That does not establish loss of all alternative epitopes or productive MHC presentation. Uptake and processing remain separate biological steps.
